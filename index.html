Frequently Asked Questions

How hard is it to install?
Connect your store, verify domain ownership using a DNS record, then install the widget script. DNS verification and script installation are separate steps. Setup time depends on your hosting provider.
What if a product detail is missing?

ClarityCart explains when the available product or store information is insufficient. It should not invent specifications, stock, prices or policies. Merchants can add missing information to improve future answers.

Will it slow down my website or break my CSS?
ClarityCart loads asynchronously and uses Shadow DOM to isolate its styles. Performance depends on the storefront and device. Test the widget on your store before publishing it.
Is my customer's data secure?
Yes. The widget requires no shopper login or personal contact details. Product questions are processed securely to generate answers and surface common shopper questions in your dashboard, and visitors are advised not to share sensitive personal information in chat.
What if it doesn't work for my store?
You have a 7-day free trial to test it completely risk-free. If it doesn't increase your conversions, you can cancel with one click.
What counts as an "AI response"?
One response equals one answer generated by the AI to a customer's question. Greeting messages don't count towards your limit.
Do you offer support?
Absolutely. We offer email support for all tiers, with priority handling for Pro and Business customers. We aim to reply within two business days.
