Frequently Asked Questions

How hard is it to install?
Super simple. You just copy and paste one line of JavaScript into your website's <head> or Google Tag Manager. No coding required.
Will it slow down my website or break my CSS?
Not at all. ClarityCart runs asynchronously and uses Shadow DOM, meaning it will never interfere with your store's speed or design.
Is my customer's data secure?
Yes. We process questions in real-time and do not store personally identifiable information (PII) from your visitors.
What if it doesn't work for my store?
You have a 7-day free trial to test it completely risk-free. If it doesn't increase your conversions, you can cancel with one click.
What counts as an "AI response"?
One response equals one answer generated by the AI to a customer's question. Greeting messages don't count towards your limit.
Do you offer support?
Absolutely. We offer email support for all tiers, with priority 24h response times for Pro and Business customers.
