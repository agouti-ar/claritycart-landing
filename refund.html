Refund Policy

Trials and renewals

Where a free trial is offered, the checkout shows its duration, the subscription price and when billing starts. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first charge. After billing starts, cancellation stops future renewals and access normally continues until the end of the paid period.

Refund requests

Paddle handles refunds for purchases made through Paddle Checkout. To request a refund, use the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit Paddle buyer support. Eligibility is governed by the Paddle Refund Policy and applicable law. This policy preserves your mandatory rights.

Manage your subscription

Use the subscription management link in your Paddle receipt to cancel. For help using ClarityCart, use our support email.
