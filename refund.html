Refund Policy

Refund Policy for B2B Subscriptions.

1. Free Trial

We provide a 7-day free trial so you can evaluate ClarityCart on your store.

2. All Sales Final

Because of the free trial period, all subscription charges are final. We do not issue refunds for "lack of ROI", change of mind, or unused time.

3. Exceptions

Refunds are issued strictly for technical failures where the widget becomes completely inoperable on supported platforms, and our support team cannot resolve the issue within 48 hours.

4. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time via the link in your billing receipt. The service will remain active until the end of the billing period.
