Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ClarityCart handles information.

1. Data Collection

We collect the information necessary to provide and secure our service, including merchant account email, connected store domain, merchant-provided store knowledge (shipping, return, and sizing policies), and the public product context of pages where the widget is installed.

2. End-User & Storefront Interaction Data

The ClarityCart widget does not require shopper accounts or intentionally request personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, emails, or payment credentials. However, free-text product questions submitted by visitors in the chat, along with anonymous widget session tokens and storefront events (such as product views and confirmed add-to-cart actions), are processed and stored so merchants can review customer questions and storefront performance. Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal data in chat messages.

3. Data Processing & Sharing

We do not sell or rent merchant or visitor data to third parties. Data is processed solely through trusted infrastructure subprocessors required to operate the service (including Supabase for encrypted database storage, Google Gemini for AI response generation, and Paddle for subscription billing).

4. Contact

We use your email and message to respond to your support request. Please leave out passwords, payment details and identity documents.

For privacy inquiries, email support.claritycart@gmail.com.
